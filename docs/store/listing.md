## English

**Short description (≤ 80 characters):** A single-player card strategy game about the Trần dynasty against the Mongol–Yuan.

**Description:**
Long Ký - The Game is a turn-based strategy game you play alone and fully offline. You lead the Trần court through the first resistance against the Mongol–Yuan invasions (1258): read the enemy's intent, hold council, give orders to your officers, evacuate the rice and choose when to strike back.

- Five Chapter 1 missions, each with objectives and star rewards.
- Keep three pillars standing: Court, Clan and People.
- Cards are stratagems and events, not playing cards.
- Vietnamese and English. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no network needed.
- Unlock extra content with codes from lessons in the Long Ký app.
